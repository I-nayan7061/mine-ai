1. Subterranean IoT (1.0 Hz)

  • Piezo-vibration RMS, kurtosis & FFT centroid

  • 24h & 72h cumulative monsoon precipitation

  • Sentinel-1 InSAR surface subsidence velocity
  • Cumulative line-of-sight vertical sag
